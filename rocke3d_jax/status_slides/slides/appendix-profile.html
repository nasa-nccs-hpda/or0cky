What this diagnosed

Every leaf function was individually @jit-decorated but called eagerly from Python — some inside nested loops (the 6-iteration Monin-Obukhov solve × 2 surface substeps = 24 dispatches on its own). Two functions never touched JAX at all.

This is what Phase 1 fused into a single jax.jit computation — the direct cause of the 62× result on the Performance slide.
