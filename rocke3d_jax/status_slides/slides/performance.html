What "62×" is actually measuring

Real Fortran SURFACE() does more physics than this JAX port's full-chain driver: proper area-weighted sub-tiling across surface types (JAX uses one dominant type/cell), the full GHY land-hydrology scheme (not wired into JAX), and a real tridiagonal turbulence solve (JAX uses a direct shortcut).

So part of 62× is JAX computing faster — and part is JAX computing less physics for that scope. A byte-for-byte faithful port would likely see a smaller ratio.
