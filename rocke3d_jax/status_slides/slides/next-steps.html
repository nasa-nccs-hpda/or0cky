Decision

Recommendation: Stop the Port Here

The speed result is real but scope-bounded. Continuing pays off only if the goal changes.

Why stop here

The goal is met

A representative workload giving consistent answers on Fortran, JAX/CPU and JAX/GPU, validated.

The rest adds fidelity, not speed evidence

Land hydrology, sea ice and turbulence are ~11,700 lines of Fortran and add scientific realism only.

They are not where the time is

Fortran spends 66% in radiation, ~10% in moist convection and ~9% in dynamics. None is ported.

When to reconsider

HQ wants JAX to run real science

Then fidelity matters: port land hydrology, sea ice and turbulence.

HQ wants a whole-model speed claim

The next investment is radiation, dynamics and moist convection: a larger, different effort than those three modules.

Not yet estimated

Effort for either path has not been scoped; we can do that on request.
