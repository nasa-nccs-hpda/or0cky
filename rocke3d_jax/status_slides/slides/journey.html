What was checked

Against the real Fortran

Kernels agree to floating-point precision (1e-9 to 1e-3), on CPU and GPU.

Against real model data

One full step correlates 0.965 with real air temperature; unchanged by either optimization.

Phase 2 vs. Phase 1

Differences are rounding-level. A few marginal cells flip a branch in an iterative solver, as they do when Phase 1 is nudged by one rounding step.

The timing itself

Two measurement bugs were found and fixed earlier, including a “CPU” baseline that was secretly the GPU.

Radiation, 66% of Fortran’s runtime, is a simplified stand-in here and is left out of the comparison.
