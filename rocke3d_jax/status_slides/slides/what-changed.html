What Changed

Two Optimizations, Two Different Ideas

Phase 1 · Sep 22

Fewer, bigger jobs

The first translation launched about 33 tiny GPU jobs per step, and launch overhead swamped the real work. We fused them into a single job.

GPU: 33 ms → 4.3 ms per step

Phase 2 · Sep 23–24

Stop moving data around

Each step still copied the model state to the GPU and back and recomputed constants. Now the state stays on the GPU across many steps, constants are computed once, and the slowest routines were rewritten (dry convection ~10× faster).

GPU: 4.3 → 0.74 ms · CPU: 17.9 → 1.9 ms

Analogy: Phase 1 combined 33 errands into one trip. Phase 2 stopped driving home between trips.

Key finding: the 5.8× GPU gain needs the “keep data on the GPU” mode. Called one step at a time, as the old interface does, the GPU gains only 1.2× (3.6 ms).
