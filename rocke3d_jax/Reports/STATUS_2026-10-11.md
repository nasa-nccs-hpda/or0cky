Owner: G. Tamkin. If this page and an older report disagree, this page is newer; the older pages are listed in section 8.
Status words: MEASURED (a run exists), HYPOTHESIS (reasoned, not run), NOT DONE.

## 1. One paragraph
A hybrid JAX model of P2SAoM40 runs two model days (108 steps) on one V100, finite at every step, starting from the real nov26 restart. The radiation is the unmodified Fortran, called live in a helper process (SOCRATES is not ported and the standing rule is that it is not modified). The dynamics, clouds and convection, surface, land (GHY and now Ent), ocean, sea ice and the daily updates run in JAX; part of the model's inputs is still read from files recorded from a real Fortran run. It is **not faster than the Fortran** (3.5 to 3.6 s per step against 2.4 s on the same node type), and the strict day-fidelity criterion is **NOT MET**.

## 5. Fidelity (scored against the five real one-ulp members, `multiday_score.py` / `d207_score.py`)
* Every run listed is finite at every step (atmosphere and ocean). The GPU result is not bitwise with the CPU or the Fortran (device exp/pow); it is judged statistically.
* **Unrelaxed day criterion (ACCEPTANCE s9): NOT MET** in every V100 run: beyond 2x the largest member distance at steps 0 to 3 in day 1 (QCI 2.37 at step 3, QCL 7.3 and U 3.4 at step 1, P 20 to 26 at step 0, where the member floor is tiny).
* **Relaxed criterion (ACCEPTANCE s10, owner decision 2026-10-11): MET** in every run (finite, and never beyond 3x at steps >= 3). The factor was chosen after seeing the numbers; it shows the run stays inside the ensemble spread at a loose margin, not agreement at rounding level.
* Day 2 alone (steps 54 to 107): never beyond 2x in any of the final runs (worst ratio 1.17 to 1.26); the error does not grow from day 1 to day 2.
* Not isolated: why the server-radiation day is worse than the replay-radiation day at steps >= 3 (candidates: computed radiation inputs, libimf cosz, hooks, live radiation).

## 6. Where speed stands and the bar
JAX is 1.45 to 1.5 times slower per step than the Fortran (3.45 to 3.56 s against 2.37 s) and 1.36 times slower per steady day. Radiation is 68 % of the Fortran step and is the same Fortran code in both runs. To beat the Fortran with the radiation retained, the JAX ordinary step work plus hand-off must fall from about 1.73 s to 0.65 to 0.75 s per step (memo section 7, with a proposed allocation, all HYPOTHESES; the cloud-base stage would have to supply about 60 % of it and no route is known: round-based loops, unrolling and XLA command-buffer flags were tried without gain). A device radiation (SOCRATES port) is necessary for a win only if the server cost is to shrink and does not touch that gap.

## 9. How to reproduce
* Frozen benchmark (3 Fortran days, then the JAX server-radiation day): `V100_CPUS=12 V100_EXTRA=--exclusive gpu/v100_run.sh bench_v100 150 'bash ../gpu/benchmark_v100.sh'` (outputs in `gpu_runs/`; protocol in `BENCHMARK_V100_PROTOCOL.md`).
* Two-day JAX run with the latest options: `ROCKE_ENT_LIVE=1 ROCKE_RADCOLS=1 ROCKE_STAGE_ARGS=1 GPU_DAYDIR=nov26_day2 GPU_DAY_DRIVER=d212_day python -u gpu_day_run.py OUTDIR --rad server --surf-fields computed --seed computed --cosz computed --nsteps 108 --timed 0` (inside `fullfidelity/`, through `gpu/v100_run.sh` on a V100 node).
* Score: `python d207_score.py --ours OUTDIR/ours_d193 --nsteps 108 --json OUT.json --md OUT.md` (`multiday_score.py --ours DIR` for 54 steps).
* Visual of the baseline for non-specialists: the published page "JAX Fortran Baseline" (https://claude.ai/artifact/YNraTfVAttFkFji4e4L3Ms, private until shared; its numbers are those of section 4 at the time of the first benchmark, before the stall fix and the later runs). `JAX_FORTRAN_BASELINE.svg` / `.png` also sit in this directory; I did not check who made them or their content.
