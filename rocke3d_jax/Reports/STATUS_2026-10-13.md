# Status of the ROCKE-3D JAX port, 2026-10-13 (supersedes `STATUS_2026-10-11.md`)

Owner: G. Tamkin. Compiled from the repository at `origin/full-fidelity-port` `e6df979` by the port-driver session; most of the work below was done by other sessions (SOCRATES, speed, port-driver) and every number is copied from the file named, not re-measured. Status words: MEASURED (a run exists), READ FROM CODE, HYPOTHESIS, NOT DONE.

## 1. One paragraph
The whole step of P2SAoM40 runs in JAX on one V100, including the radiation: the SOCRATES port (`--rad native`, `fullfidelity/socrates_spike/`) replaces the Fortran radiation helper, and with the standalone flags the run reads only five step-0 dump files (about 278 MB) and no per-step records (five-day, 240-step run finished and finite). Against the one-core Fortran on the same node type it is at **parity** in the frozen release configuration (0.95 to 0.97 of the Fortran seconds per step) and **4.4 times faster** per steady step in the speed configuration (0.524 against 2.285 s per step, one run). It is nov26 only. The strict day-fidelity criterion is **still NOT MET**; the relaxed one is MET. The cold start is 925 to 1,200 s.

## 6. Fidelity (five real one-ulp members, `d207_score.py`)
* All runs finite at every step. GPU results are not bitwise with the CPU or Fortran.
* **Unrelaxed criterion (ACCEPTANCE s9): NOT MET** in every configuration. Day 1 worst ratio at steps >= 3: QCI 2.37 at step 3 (release), **2.47 in the speed configuration** (same step and field; the owner accepted this shift for now on 2026-10-13); QCL 1.58 at step 3.
* **Relaxed criterion (s10): MET.** Day 2 never beyond 2x (worst 1.10 to 1.22 in the release runs, 1.11 in the speed run).
* The merged `full-fidelity-port` 54-step check (job 37942050, flags off): same worst ratios as the release; within/near counts differ from the scratch-merge run, cause not investigated; raw `FSF` arrays in `native_rad_outputs.npz` differ between 54-step runs by 150 to 460 W/m2 and were not traced.
* Radiation alone: 72 packets of a free-running 30 days sent to the unmodified Fortran server, no excursion above 1e-6 W/m2 (largest FSF 3.3e-9, TRHR 5.4e-8); 365 daily dates at rounding level (SRHR <= 9.8e-9, TRHR <= 1.8e-7). Not a comparison of trajectories.

## 7. Known problems and open items
* **QDYNAM extra-column branch (`qdynam_do_z_extra`)**: fired on 24 steps (1288 to 1306, 1332 to 1336) of the 30-day trial; the port does not execute that branch, so states from step 1288 on are not valid. A rerun with the dump (D231) did not reproduce it in 1440 steps; the cause is NOT identified.
* `mask_mismatch` rose from 0 to 9 by step 1439 of the 30-day trial (first at the day-240 boundary); meaning not investigated.
* The first 30-day run's steps took 7.5 to 8 s against 2.7 to 3.2 s in the rerun; shadow dump or a shared node, not isolated.
* GPU `ROCKE_STAGE_ARGS=1` is not bitwise equal to the flag-off result (rounding level).
* The `interp_aer` Fortran edit (about a third of RADIA in the Fortran itself, recorded in the SOCRATES report) is NOT decided; A100 / float32 and AIJ are not done.
* Regression with flags off: 3,329 to 3,342 passed, 1 to 2 failed (the known order-dependent libimf test; the two untracked `jax_imf`/`jax_state` tests as of 2026-10-11), 16 skipped. The new flags are covered by targeted tests and V100 runs only.
