## 1. Result in one paragraph
Demonstrated: at step 31 (second surface substep) the port's downdraft flag of land cell 112 is on (ddml 1, PBL gusti 1.835) while the real model has ddml 0 there. `jax_surface._land` builds the GHY forcing from the RECORDED ffg batch and overrides ts, qs, rho, ch, vs, tprime, qprime, qm1 with the port's PBL values, but NOT `gusti`: GHY keeps the recorded gusti_in (0.0). The Fortran passes `pbl_args%gusti` (GHY_DRV.f:1267), and the real records agree bitwise (ffg col 160 == ffp col 113 on every land cell). The port therefore feeds GHY a PBL surface humidity `qsrf` that contains the downdraft term `gusti*(qtop-qdns)/ws` (0.0195 against ground saturation 0.0168 and air 0.0155) WITHOUT the compensating `-gusti*qprime` in the potential evaporation: the potential flux epb/epv is a strong condensation, aevap = -0.2616 kg/m2 in one iteration (real +4.2e-3), ashg +110665 (real -1872), alhg -653939 (real ~ +1e4): the ground is heated by a 727 W/m2 latent release and the explicit one-iteration step then runs away. With the PBL gusti given to GHY, the same single-cell call gives aevap +5.7e-3, ashg -5912. A day run with the one-line consistent forcing (`--gustifix 1`, on top of the D199 elhx fix) is finite in all 7 fields to step 44 (ON: NaN from 39). A second, separate, demonstrated mismatch is the GHY sub-iteration schedule (recorded nit/dts replayed instead of the Fortran `gdtm` loop); it amplifies once the state is perturbed (section 5) but did not start this runaway.

## 4. First departing quantity (task 2), step 31 substep 2, single cell, same inputs
Everything up to the GHY forcing is the PBL's output on the port's own atmosphere (PBL is validated against the real records for real inputs; not re-tested here). In GHY, the first quantity that departs is the potential evaporation of the first iteration: evapb/evapvw -2.77e-7 / -2.91e-7 m/s with the recorded gusti against +1.98e-8 / +6.4e-9 with the PBL gusti (`d202_diag.py`). That is the evaporation/condensation potential, fed by the forcing; it is not the nit schedule (nit = 1 in both), not the surface-layer stability branch (the PBL output is unchanged between the two calls), and not the `evap_min` limiter (not binding). It explains the sign flip of aevap, the latent release (alhg -653939 J/m2 = 727 W/m2) and the ashg of +110665.
Why only here and why the PBL output looks odd: the PBL humidity `qsrf` is the first-level value of its prognostic q profile; with ddml it is driven by `usurf*qgrnd - gusti*(qdns-qtop)` (pbl_ff.q_eqn), so `qsrf = qg - gusti*qprime/ws` = 0.0168 + 1.835*0.0029/1.94 = 0.0195. GHY subtracts `gusti*qprime` again in epb/epv (ghy_jax.evap_limits, GHY.f:904), which cancels exactly that term when it gets the same gusti.
Why the port's gusti differs from the record: `cell_inputs` computes ddml/gusti from the port's own DDMS/DDML (condensation state), which departs from the real one in a free-running day; the GHY template keeps the recorded value. The inconsistency is therefore silent while the port is near the real state (identical at step 0; ON equals the fixed run bitwise at step 0).

## 5. Second finding: the GHY sub-iteration schedule is replayed, not derived (`ghy_ref_nit` replay; also d202_fix.py)
The Fortran time loop (GHY.f:2389-2416) takes dts = min(dtm, dtr/2) from `gdtm` of the CURRENT state (stability limit 0.5*ak2/xk2, xk2 = sha*rho*ch*vs + ...). The port takes nsub/edts from the real record (`ffnit`). Replay B (NumPy, Fortran loop `use_recorded_dts=False`) against replay A (recorded schedule, = the port): at 30/1 recorded nit 1, gdtm nit 3 (dtm0 381 s), aevap 3.6e-2 against 4.3e-2; at 32/2 recorded nit 1, gdtm nit 5 (dtm0 152 s), with the state already perturbed: ashg +112929 (port, explicit step of 900 s at ch 0.0625) against -2268 (gdtm schedule, same gusti), aevap 0.164 against 0.096. So once the ground is hot and `ch*vs` is large, the recorded one-iteration schedule is beyond the explicit-Euler stability limit and amplifies, as in D201 H2 (partly confirmed: it is an amplifier, not the origin; at 31/2 itself the schedule is irrelevant, nit 8 vs 1 changes aevap from -0.26 to -0.147 only, still wrong, while gusti alone gives +5.7e-3).
`d202_fix.advnc_gdtm` implements the loop in the JAX scan (gdtm batched, same signature as `ghy_jax.advnc`, width 40, Ent of iteration i = recorded min(i, W0-1) as `ghy_ref_nit`). Validation on REAL records (no port state), all 753 land cells: step 14 sub 1, step 17 sub 2, step 30 sub 1 and 2: the computed nit equals the recorded ffnit in 753/753 cells each, none exhausted; outputs equal the recorded-schedule `advnc` to 1e-13 (tsns 0.0 / bitwise in tsns, 1e-10 in alhg) (`fix_test_step*.json`). It is not bitwise to the recorded-schedule run (dts is recomputed), and it costs the full width (40 lanes) in every call.
