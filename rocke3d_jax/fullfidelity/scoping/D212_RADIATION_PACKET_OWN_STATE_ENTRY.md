* How each input is built (hooks live in the subclass `CoupledD209` of d212_day.py; no jax_* file touched):
  1. **COSZ1** on held steps: `drv_zenith.Zenith().cosz1(itime)` (libimf), substituted into `rec['rad']['COSZ1']` in a wrapper of `ph.load_records`. On radiation steps COSZ1 is the server's.
  2. **Seed**: the D174 LCG chain (`drv_rng.radia_seed`, `drv_rng.next_seed`) from the ONE recorded value `SEEDS[0]` of the first step (not in the restart), converted to the signed int32 convention of the record; substituted into `rec['seed']`.
  3. **21 surface fields**, at the start of each radiation step from OUR state (the same state the step uses):
     * ice/lake/land-ice group (RSI ZSI SNOWI POND_MELT FLAG_DSWS FLAKE DLAKE FLICE FLAND FEARTH GTEMPR1 GTEMPR2 GTEMPR3 SNOWLI ZSNOWI): our MELT_SI of this step (`jax_p1_melt.melt_si`), `jax_seaice_lake.seaice_to_atmgrid`, `jax_radiation.surface_fields_jax`, with the live statics (`cp.st`, so the day-boundary FLAKE/FEARTH change from daily_LAKE is picked up);
     * land group GTEMPR4 BARESW SNOWD FRSNOW: `drv_radpacket.land_fields` on our carried GHY state of the last substep (`land_prev`: tbcs, w, nsn, dzsn, wsn, fr_snow), with `snowbv` carried from the restart value through every step (updated after every step, as GHY does); step 0: the restart arrays (`drv_radpacket.restart_land_arrays`);
     * TSAVG: our composite `S['TSAVG']`; WSAVG: sum over types of ftype*ws of OUR last PBL substep (captured from the stage output by wrapping `jax_surface.make_stage`; the wrapper only stores the outputs); step 0: restart `tsavg`/`wsavg`.
     * New static record reads (declared, not state): the cell index, `top_dev`, `fv` and soil columns (for BARESW = w/(thets*dz)) of the land rows of `ffg_<it>.bin` (second half of the previous step; first half at step 0). These are static soil/vegetation columns, not state, but they ARE a recorded read.
