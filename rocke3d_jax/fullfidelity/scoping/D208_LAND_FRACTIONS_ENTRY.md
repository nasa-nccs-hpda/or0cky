## 2. What was ported
`daily_land_fractions.update_land_fractions(S, thm0)`: statement order and expressions of the Fortran (scalar loop over the land rows; arithmetic in IEEE float64 order). Shrink (flake < svflake): underwater state mixed into bare and vegetated fractions, canopy water scaled, `fr_snow *= 1 - dfrac/fearth`. Expansion: layers 6..1, `dw = min(dfrac*w_stor(k), sum_water + dw_soil)`, `w_stor(1) = 1e30`, heat of the lake water `DGML*BYAXYP/sum_water` per m3, canopy water dumped into layer 1, `fr_snow *= 1 + dfrac/fearth` with the 0.95 cap for fb or fv = 0 and the stop conditions as `RuntimeError` (the Fortran `stop_model`). Not ported, by reason: water tracers (not compiled in); `snow_cover` into `atmlnd%fr_snow_rad` (radiation input, radiation replayed); `set_new_ghy_cells_outputs` (output arrays qg/tearth/tsns/wearth/aiearth of cells that had NO land before; not part of the carried state; rows where it would act are returned in `info['new_cell_rows']`: **empty** at the nov26 boundary); `remove_extra_snow_to_ocean` (wsn_max = 0, off). No JAX version (once per day, 753 rows, data-dependent loop, no fidelity gain).

## 3. The hook (`jax_coupled.Coupled.daily_lake_update(state, land_fractions=True)`)
After the NumPy `daily_lake` of D205, with DMWLDF/DGML/SVFLAKE it returned (DMWLDF as modified by daily_LAKE), the transfer is applied to `state['land_prev']['dyn_next']` (w, ht, fr_snow; the carried GHY state the next step starts from) for the 753 land rows. `land_prev['ghy']` (raw output of the last step) is left unchanged. The underwater fraction (ibv = 3) is NOT carried by the land chain: at the first boundary its state is taken from the recorded rows of the last step (cols 9-50, **a record feed**; it is constant since the restart because nothing but this transfer changes it: w3 equal at steps 0 and 47 in the record, max diff 0.0), afterwards from `self._land3`, which the transfer updates (multi-day capable, untested beyond one boundary). Default on: validated as in section 4. `d193_day.main` still not wired (as D205); `d208_day.py` wraps it (`--land-fractions 0|1`). Info: `info['land_fractions']` = 293 shrunk, 338 expanded, `new_cell_rows` [], 0 changed cells with fearth > 0 but no land row.
Declared still not applied: `atmlnd` snow-rad fields, RESET_SURF_FLUXES, MDWNIMP/EDWNIMP into daily_LI, daily_EARTH vegetation/albedo items (D206 gap list).

This is trajectory error of our land water, not a formula error (fed the real water, the DMWLDF transcription gives the layer-1 saturation seen in the real record: all six underwater layers end exactly at storage in the four cells inspected, whose implied real lake water agrees with the formula to 0.1-3%; the zero-difference result in the table uses it).
