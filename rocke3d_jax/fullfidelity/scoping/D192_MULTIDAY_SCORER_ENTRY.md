## What it does
Input: a per-step end-state sequence of our run (T U V Q P QCL QCI, the layout of `ours_<tag>/step_<it>.npz` and of `arrays['filter']` of `Coupled.step(keep=True)`). For every step k it computes the whole-column rms of (ours - real) with `atm_day_report.state_metrics`, the noise floor N(k) = [min, max] over the five real one-ulp members p1..p5 of rms(member_k - real_k) (as `atm_day_report.curves` does, reference = the real step-end state), and classifies with the unchanged `atm_day_report.overlay/classify`: ratio = ours/max member; within <= 1, near <= 2, beyond > 2, below (within and ours < 0.5 min member; counted as within). Per field it reports class per step, counts, near/beyond step lists, the worst ratio over all steps and over steps >= 3 (the D157/D171 convention; the floor at k < 3 is ~1e-9), plus zonal-mean rms and |global-mean| counts. Day-level flags are separate: (a) within at every step (b) never beyond 2x the largest member; (b) is also given for steps >= 3 only. The scorer reports the facts and does not relax a criterion; the verdict text says which part holds.
CLI: `python multiday_score.py --ours <dir> [--nsteps 54] [--cache members.json] [--json] [--md]`; `--chain-npz '<glob>'` for d191_run.validate npz files ('filter/T' keys).
Adapter: `chain_states(list_of_arrays | list_of_npz | glob)` returns a provider; a sequence with fewer than 54 steps is labelled "SHORT WINDOW ... not the one-model-day criterion of ACCEPTANCE 4" in the label and the verdict. Note: `d191_chain.py` as written writes only the C1 comparison JSON, not states; to score its 6 steps, the loop must keep `arrays['filter']` of each step (or the `*_label.npz` of `d191_run.validate`). No such run was made here and no edit of `d191_chain.py` was made. The adapter was tested on the existing nov26 day files in the same layout (6-step window equals the corresponding steps of the 54-step score).
Every report carries the sentence "radiation computed by the original Fortran (hybrid component)".

## Not done / limitations
- The 54-step coupled day (jax_coupled) was not run (no such run exists); no longer window; the 8 JAN1950 members and the D172 leave-one-out scoring for windows beyond a day are not implemented. 5 members, one start state, one day: the heavy-tailed condensates (QCI, QCL) are single-cell dominated (D157), so a ratio near 2 is not a significance statement.
- Only the 6-step window of `d191_chain.py` can be scored when its states are kept; at 6 steps only k = 3..5 enter the "steps >= 3" ratio, and the floor at k < 3 is ~1e-9.
- The scorer needs the ff_data real states and member files (`ffa_step_<it>_e.bin`, `ffpt_*`); it does not compare layer-level or flux-level statistics.
- Test run: only `tests/test_multiday_score.py`, cores 3-5, 12 passed (data tests about 60 s). No other tests run.
