## 2. What was ported (`daily_lake.py`)
`daily_lake` (all branches of LAKES.f:2492-3010 for the P2SAoM40 build), `cubicroot` (shared/CubicEquation_mod.F90), `newton` (LAKES.f:4059; only reached with Power_law_lakes > 0, ported but NOT exercised), `water_deficit` (GHY_DRV.f:4054-4129 `compute_water_deficit` = DMWLDF), `tanlk`/`lake_statics` (LAKES.f:670-690, 770-784). Parameters (read from source, not assumed): variable_lk = 1 (P2SAoM40.R:176), MINMLD = 1, HLAKE_MIN = 1, lake_ice_max = 5 (default, < 1e10: ice goes to the implicit MDWNIMP/EDWNIMP arrays), Power_law_lakes = 0, small_lake_evap = 0, Cael_lakes = 0 (all LAKES.f defaults, the rundeck sets none of them). Real exponents through the Intel libimf `pow` (`intel_libm_ff`), numpy `pow` as fallback (reported in `out['pow']`).
Statement order of the Fortran is kept (scalar loop over the cells). Not ported, by reason: water tracers (not compiled in), diagnostics (INC_AJ/INC_AREG/AIJ), `RESET_SURF_FLUXES` (edits FSF/TRSURF of the radiation restart arrays; radiation is replayed; the calls are listed in `out['reset_surf_fluxes']`, 0 effect on state), `read_irrig` (IRRIGATION_ON not in the build list; not checked beyond `decks/P2SAoM40.mk`), HALO_UPDATEs. JAX version (`jax_daily_lake.py`): NOT written. Reason: it runs once per model day on 753 land/lake cells with data-dependent branches and a host cubic solve; the declared host round trip (as `daily_on_state` for DAILY_ATMDYN) costs 0.7 s plus the statics rebuild; a device version would add no fidelity and its validation would repeat section 3.
Inputs the Fortran takes from other modules and where they come from here: TANLK from FLAKE0 (TOPO file) and HLAKE (TOPO `hlake`, clamped >= 1 m where FLAKE0 + FLAKE > 0; equals the recorded ffl2 HLAKE on all lake cells, difference 0.0); AXYP = DXYPO(j); FLICE, FOCEAN from the statics; **DMWLDF** is computed from the soil water of the LAST land step: our carried `land_prev['dyn_next']['w']` (device) with dz, q and Ent fv from the recorded ffg rows of that step (THM(0,:) of `ghy_ref`).

## 4. The hook (`jax_coupled.py`, the only tracked file changed; +88/-7 lines)
`Coupled.daily_lake_update(state)`: ONE declared host round trip (lake, ice, atm-grid lake exports and the soil water of the last land step device -> host), `daily_lake.daily_lake`, device state replaced (ice rsi/msi/snowi/hsi, lake mwl/gml/tlake/mldlk, atm gtemp/gtempr/mlhc at lake cells), then the static lake fractions are rebuilt: `st['fland'|'fearth']`, geometry (`make_geo`), `K`/`Kb` (`make_static_all`), `static181`/`sp181`, `melt_geo`; the jitted `pre`/`pa` are rebuilt and the SURFACE stage cache dropped (step 48 wall 110 s: 9 compiles). Also: `_build_jits(only=...)` factored out of `__init__`, and `step()` stores `self._last_ghy` (the recorded ffg rows and cells of the step, for DMWLDF). The ocean jits (`oa`, `ob`) and `pb` are deliberately NOT rebuilt: they read only focean/dxypo/oc/valid/adv/is_ocean (no lake fraction), and re-tracing `ob` raised `UnexpectedTracerError` in run 1 (`ocean_ofluxv.py:27` builds module-level jnp constants ZE/DZO at its first import, which happens inside the first trace of `ob`; latent bug in an untouched file, not fixed). Order as the Fortran `daily_atm`: after DAILY_ATMDYN/ch4ox (`d193_day.daily_on_state`), before the step. `d205_day.py` wraps `d193_day.daily_on_state` and `Coupled` (no edit of `d193_day.py`).
**Declared NOT applied at the boundary** (in `info['not_applied']`): the GHY water/heat transfer for the changed lake fraction (GHY_DRV.f:4531 `sum_water = DMWLDF*dfrac/rhow`, uses DMWLDF, DGML, SVFLAKE; the arrays are returned in `d205_lake_hook.npz` for the land owner; the land state of step 48 is therefore still missing this transfer), RESET_SURF_FLUXES, MDWNIMP/EDWNIMP into daily_LI, daily_EARTH.
