The tests were written on a non-AVX-512 host. The JAX runs on the same nodes use host NumPy too; whether that moves the fidelity ratios was not tested.

## 5b. SME review comment and the per-level zonal check (2026-10-20, day 1 only)

SME comment (2026-10-20, on the day-1 comparison): the differences look like noise and sit reasonably within the ensemble of Fortran runs; one thing caught their eye, a "wave-like" structure above about layer 25 in the zonal-mean JAX-Fortran U and T, possibly a small difference in vertical structure; hard to say after one day, watch how it evolves.

Check made: `fullfidelity/zonal_vertical_check.py` on run e (release and fast), zonal mean per level, JAX-Fortran against the five one-ulp members' differences from the Fortran control. Ratio per level = rms over latitude of the JAX difference / largest member rms (inspection tool, no acceptance threshold). Method, command, data and the per-step numbers: `Reports/zonal_check_20261020/` (README.txt, `zonal_vertical_<kind>.txt`, plots).
* From step 11 to step 53 the upper levels (26-40) are within the members: fast median ratio T 0.80-0.86, U 0.83-1.03; maximum 0.97-1.30; no level beyond 2. Release is similar. The fast step-53 U map shows the same structure for member p1 (only that map was viewed as an image).
* At steps 1-3 upper-level T (and some U) is beyond the members: fast median T 3.31 at step 1 and 1.90 at step 3, maximum 6.43 at level 38 (step 1); U maximum 3.46 at level 26 (step 3). This is the same early-step excess behind the failed steps 0-2 of the approved rule; its cause is not identified.
* Day 2 (steps 53, 54, 59, 71, 89, 107, from the run-e timing-pair states; addendum in the same folder): no growing upper-level offset. Fast upper-level median ratio U 0.69-0.94, T 0.76-1.06; maximum 1.02-1.26 (fast), 0.78-1.26 (release); no level beyond 2. Only six steps and only U and T were sampled; the members continue their day-1 curves; the day-2 plots were not viewed as images.
* Limits: steps 0-107 only (two days; day 1 in detail, day 2 at six steps); nothing later checked, so later evolution is unknown; not area-weighted; this does not resolve the strict-rule failure (section 5) and says nothing about longer runs.
